MARCELLO by NOMAN

Studio App Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes the intended data practices of MARCELLO by NOMAN Studio, a private Pinterest integration operated by MARCELLO by NOMAN for its own business account. The app is being prepared for API access and is not offered to external customers.

Information the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app, it may access the connected Pinterest account's identifier and profile, its own boards and Pins, and available account and Pin performance metrics. It may store OAuth access and refresh tokens, board and Pin identifiers, product images, titles, descriptions, destination links, publishing schedules, and limited operational records such as publishing results and errors.

The app does not request Pinterest passwords, access other users' private boards, or collect Etsy customer orders, payment information, or buyer messages.

How information is used

Information is used to create and schedule original product Pins, organize the owner's boards, link Pins to the Etsy shop, prevent duplicate publishing, troubleshoot failures, and assess the performance of the owner's content. The app is intended for the shop owner's use only.

Sharing and public content

Images, text, and links selected for publication are sent to Pinterest and become visible according to the destination board and Pinterest's settings. Pinterest processes information under its Privacy Policy. Visiting a destination Etsy listing is subject to Etsy's own privacy practices.

App data is not sold, rented, used to train general-purpose AI models, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. Technical service providers used to operate the integration may process data only as needed to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

Storage and security

The integration is intended to run in an owner-controlled environment. Credentials must be kept outside public files and repositories, with access restricted to the operator. No access tokens are published on this policy page. No system can guarantee complete security.

Retention and deletion

Publishing records and configuration are retained only while needed to operate the integration. On discontinuation or a verified deletion request, the operator will remove stored tokens and associated Pinterest account data from active app storage within 30 days, unless retention is legally required. Any retained backup copy will remain restricted until it is overwritten or removed within 90 days.

Revoking the app's access stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete previously published Pins. The owner can manage or delete published content directly on Pinterest. Pinterest and Etsy independently control data held by their services.

Your choices and contact

The connected account owner may revoke the integration through Pinterest's connected-app settings. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or this policy, contact MARCELLO by NOMAN through the seller-contact option at our Etsy shop. Please identify the request as relating to the Studio app and do not send passwords or access tokens.

Policy page visitors

This page contains no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, cookies set by the page, or information-submission forms. The hosting provider may process standard request information, including IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time, for delivering and protecting the page.

Changes

Changes to app data practices will be reflected here with an updated effective date before new practices take effect. Expansion to external users or additional data uses requires an updated policy and appropriate authorization.
